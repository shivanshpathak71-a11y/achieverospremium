# Shivansh App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

## Overview

Shivansh — Income Tax Officer ("the App") is a personal brand learning platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (stored as a secure hash). This is used only for authentication and account management.

### Study Progress
We store your course progress, bookmarks, and study streaks. This data is linked to your account and is used only to display your personal progress.

### Usage Data
The app may collect anonymous usage analytics (such as which features are used) to improve the app. No personally identifiable information is shared with third parties.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the app's features (courses, lectures, progress tracking)
- To authenticate you and keep your data secure
- To improve the app's content and user experience

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (a PostgreSQL-based backend) with row-level security policies. Only you can access your own study data.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses the following services:
- **Supabase** — for authentication and data storage
- **Google Fonts** — for typography

No advertising SDKs or tracking services are included.

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

## Your Rights

- You can access your study data at any time within the app
- You can request a copy of your data
- You can request deletion of your account and data

## Children's Privacy

The app is intended for adults (18+) preparing for competitive examinations. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app.
